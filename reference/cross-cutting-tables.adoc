== Cross-cutting tables

=== Materials (`-03` and trade designations)

Append after head code via `-03` (UDC) followed by alloy / polymer / species designation.

[%header,cols="2,3"]
|===
| Notation | Material

| `-034.1`                                | Ferrous metal
| `-034.4`                                | Aluminum
| `-034.5`                                | Copper-base alloy
| `-035.5`                                | Wood
| `-037`                                  | Plastic
| `-034.1'1018`                           | AISI/SAE 1018 carbon steel
| `-034.1'4140`                           | AISI/SAE 4140 alloy steel
| `-034.1'304L`, `'316L`                  | AISI 300-series stainless
| `-034.4'6061-T6`, `'7075-T651`          | Aluminum Association
| `-034.5'C36000`, `'C26000`              | UNS brass
| `-037'PETG`, `'PLA`, `'ABS` …           | ISO 1043-1 polymer abbreviations
|===

=== Place auxiliary `(...)`

Local convention: `(<bay>.<bin>)` for shop locations; `(US-<state>-<room>)` for cross-property reference.

=== Time auxiliary `"..."`

ISO 8601 form. `"2024-08"` month-precision acquisition; `"2025-12-15"` exact.
Useful for batch/expiration on chemicals, adhesives, films, charts.

=== Language auxiliary `=...`

ISO 639-3 codes. `=eng`, `=fra`, `=deu`, `=jpn`.
Useful for boxed games or instruments with bundled documentation in a specific language.
