== Photography

FSC 6720, 6730, 6740, 6750, 6760, 6780.

[%header,cols="1,3"]
|===
| Code | Sub-class

| 6720.1 | Digital camera body
| 6720.2 | Film camera body
| 6720.3 | Instant camera
| 6740.1 | Enlarger
| 6740.2 | Developing tank / reels
| 6740.3 | Trays / safelights
| 6750.1 | Film stock
| 6750.2 | Photographic chemistry
| 6750.3 | Photographic paper
| 6760.1 | Lens
| 6760.2 | Filter
| 6760.3 | Tripod
| 6760.4 | Tripod head / gimbal
| 6760.5 | Memory card
| 6760.6 | Battery / charger
| 6760.7 | Lighting (strobe, continuous, modifier)
|===

Lens mount via `'` from de facto industry names: `'F` Nikon F, `'EF` Canon EF, `'RF` Canon RF, `'E` Sony E/FE, `'X` Fuji X, `'MFT` Micro Four Thirds, `'Z` Nikon Z, `'L` L-mount, `'K` Pentax K.
Filter thread in mm via `'<size>`.
Film format via `'135`, `'120`, `'4x5`, `'8x10`.
ISO speed via `'ISO<n>`.

.Example
[example]
====
`6760.1'EF 50mm-f1.8 (D9.B3)`

Canon EF mount, 50 mm f/1.8.
====
