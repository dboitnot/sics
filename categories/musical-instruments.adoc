== Musical instruments

FSC 7710 Musical instruments; FSC 7720 Musical instrument parts and accessories.

Top-level via Hornbostel-Sachs (1914), attached as `7710'<H-S code>`.
The H-S numbers themselves are the sub-class.

[%header,cols="1,3"]
|===
| H-S | Top category

| 1 | Idiophones
| 2 | Membranophones
| 3 | Chordophones
| 4 | Aerophones
| 5 | Electrophones (Sachs 1940)
|===

H-S subdivides decimally (e.g. 321.322 = necked lute, plucked, with mechanical action).
Use as much depth as needed.

.Example
[example]

Electric guitar (chordophone, lute-type, plucked).
