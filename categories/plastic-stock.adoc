== Plastic stock and 3D printer filament

FSC 9330 Plastic Fabricated Materials.

[%header,cols="1,3"]
|===
| Code | Sub-class

| 9330.1   | Filament (3D printing)
| 9330.11  | 1.75 mm diameter
| 9330.12  | 2.85 mm diameter
| 9330.2   | Sheet
| 9330.3   | Rod
| 9330.4   | Tube
| 9330.5   | Granulate / pellets
| 9330.6   | Adhesive-backed sheet
|===

Polymer code via `'` from ISO 1043-1: `'PETG`, `'PLA`, `'ABS`, `'PC`, `'POM`, `'PTFE`, `'PEEK`, `'HDPE`, `'LDPE`, `'PP`, `'PVC`, `'PMMA`, `'TPU`, `'PA`.

.Example
[example]
====
`9330.11'PETG (B5.B1) "2025-12"`

1.75 mm PETG filament, opened December 2025.
====
