== Notation reference

[%header,cols="1,2,4"]
|===
| Operator | Name | Use

| `.0`–`.9`
| Decimal subdivision
| Numeric hierarchy under a class, e.g. `5305.12`

| `'`
| Synthesis special auxiliary
| Combines a concept from outside the class, e.g. `9330.1'PETG`

| `+`
| Coordination
| Two equally-ranked concepts

| `:`
| Relation
| Generic non-symmetric link between concepts

| `/`
| Range
| Span between two notations

| `-03`
| Material (UDC Table 1g)
| e.g. `-034` metal, `-037` plastic

| `(...)`
| Place (UDC Table 1e)
| e.g. `(D3.B2)` drawer 3 bin 2

| `"..."`
| Time (UDC Table 1c)
| e.g. `"2024-08"` for batch or acquisition

| `=...`
| Language (UDC Table 1b)
| e.g. `=eng` English-language packaging
|===
