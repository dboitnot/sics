== Metal stock

[%header,cols="1,3"]
|===
| Code | Sub-class

| 9510.1 | Round bar (ferrous)
| 9510.2 | Square bar
| 9510.3 | Hex bar
| 9510.4 | Rectangular (flat) bar
| 9515.1 | Plate (≥ 6 mm)
| 9515.2 | Sheet
| 9515.3 | Strip
| 9515.4 | Foil
| 9520.1 | Angle
| 9520.2 | Channel
| 9520.3 | I/W beam
| 9520.4 | Round tube/pipe
| 9520.5 | Square/rectangular tube
| 9520.6 | T-bar
| 9520.7 | Z-bar
| 9530.1–.4 | Round, square, hex, rectangular bar (nonferrous)
| 9535.1–.4 | Plate, sheet, strip, foil (nonferrous)
|===

Alloy designation attaches via `'` from open standards: AISI/SAE for steel (`'1018`, `'4140`, `'304L`, `'316L`); Aluminum Association (`'6061-T6`, `'7075-T651`); UNS for copper alloys (`'C36000`, `'C26000`).

.Example
[example]
====
`9510.1'1018 1/2"x36" (S2.R3)`

1018 cold-rolled round bar, 1/2″ × 36″.
====
