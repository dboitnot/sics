== Paints, coatings, adhesives

FSC 8010 Paints, dopes, varnishes; FSC 8030 Preservative and sealing compounds; FSC 8040 Adhesives.

[%header,cols="1,3"]
|===
| Code | Sub-class

| 8010.1 | Paint, finish coat
| 8010.2 | Primer
| 8010.3 | Lacquer / shellac / varnish
| 8010.4 | Stain
| 8010.5 | Specialty (high-heat, marine, anti-slip)
| 8030.1 | Sealant (silicone, polyurethane, butyl)
| 8030.2 | Threadlocker (anaerobic)
| 8030.3 | Anti-corrosion
| 8030.4 | Wood preservative
| 8040.1 | Epoxy
| 8040.2 | Cyanoacrylate
| 8040.3 | PVA wood glue
| 8040.4 | Contact cement
| 8040.5 | Polyurethane construction
| 8040.6 | Hot-melt
| 8040.7 | Spray adhesive
|===

Color via SAE-AMS-STD-595 five-digit codes attached as `'<code>` (e.g. `'17875` gloss insignia white).
First digit: 1 gloss, 2 semi-gloss, 3 flat.
Second digit: 0 brown, 1 red, 2 orange, 3 yellow, 4 green, 5 blue, 6 grey, 7 misc, 8 fluorescent.
