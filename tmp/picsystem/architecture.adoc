== Architecture

Four layers.

. *Layer 1*, the FSC four-digit class, is the bin.
. *Layer 2* is the UDC notation grammar (operators only, not the schedules).
. *Layer 3* is the UDC common auxiliaries: material `-03`, place `(...)`, time `"..."`, language `=...`.
. *Layer 4* is locally-defined numeric sub-classes under each FSC, using named codes only where a public standard supplies them.

Time and language auxiliaries are part of the system and available whenever useful (acquisition or batch dates, language of bundled documentation), even if rarely needed for objects.
