== Limitations

FSC fits some modern objects poorly: smartphones at 5820, hobby drones at 1550 (formally restricted to military UAS).
Documented on the reference sheet rather than reclassified.

ASME B18, ASTM E438/E960, ISO 128/216/269/1043-1/1222/5457, IEC 60320 are paywalled.
Designations are factual; substantive prose is not reproduced.

The Sheffield gouge list is a de facto convention, not a strict standard.
Pfeil, Ashley Iles, Henry Taylor, Two Cherries, Auriou, Stubai deviate at the margins.
Manufacturer convention recorded per tool.

The 2011 MIMO H-S revision PDF carries no Creative Commons license.
Numerical structure is reusable as a system; prose is not, and is paraphrased here.

The 1961 Baines-Wachsmann translation in _Galpin Society Journal_ 14 remains under copyright.
Numerical codes are factual; prose is not.

BoardGameGeek taxonomy is proprietary.
Game names and publishers are factual; the curated category and mechanism taxonomy is not.

LiPo C-ratings are routinely inflated by manufacturers.
Record the nameplate but plan motor and ESC sizing on measured current draw.

FAA aeronautical chart cycle is 56 days as of 25 February 2021.
Charts have currency dates; record via `"..."` time auxiliary.
