== Abstract

A paper-based, four-layer classification for the physical objects in a home workshop, hobby room, lab, garden, and office.
Layer 1 is the U.S. Defense Logistics Agency Federal Supply Classification (FSC).
Layer 2 is the Universal Decimal Classification (UDC) notation grammar.
Layer 3 is a selection of UDC common auxiliary tables.
Layer 4 is locally-defined sub-class tables sourced from open standards where they exist.
Documents are out of scope; they live in a separate Dewey-derivative system.
